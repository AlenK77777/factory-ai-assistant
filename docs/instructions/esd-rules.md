# WI-GEN-002 ESD Rules in Protected Areas

> Synthetic, for demo only. Invented for a home-lab project; not a real procedure.

Revision: A. Owner: Facilities and Quality. Applies to: the ESD protected areas (EPA) at Electrical Test, Final QA and Packing.

## Why it matters

Several Defense, Aerospace and Medical customers require that finished boards are handled under ESD control. Static discharge can damage embedded components and leaves no visible mark, so the rules below are mandatory for everyone who enters an EPA, including visitors.

## Before entering the EPA

1. Wear an ESD smock, fully closed, and ESD shoes or heel straps on both feet.
2. Test your wrist strap and heel straps at the tester at the EPA entrance at the start of every shift. Write the result in the ESD test log.
3. If a test fails, do not enter. Replace the strap and test again. Report repeated failures to your shift manager.

## Working in the EPA

- Connect your wrist strap to the bench grounding point whenever you sit at an ESD bench.
- Keep personal items (plastic bottles, phones in plastic cases, bags, paper) at least 30 cm away from boards, or outside the EPA.
- Move boards only in ESD-safe bins, racks or shielding bags. Never stack bare boards in ordinary plastic boxes.
- Hold boards by the edges. Do not touch pads or gold fingers.
- Relative humidity must stay between 30% and 70%. If the room display shows a value outside this range, tell the shift manager.

## Checks and records

- Facilities checks bench and floor resistance once a month and labels each bench with the check date.
- A bench with an expired label must not be used until it is checked again.
- Report any ESD event (spark, shock, damaged strap) in the MES quality log, with the order ID of the boards involved.
